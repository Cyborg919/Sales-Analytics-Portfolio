# Sales Analytics Dashboard

A portfolio-ready sales analytics project built from a synthetic Indian retail dataset.

## Goal
Analyze sales, profitability, customers, products, regions, channels, discounts, returns, and customer satisfaction.

## Dataset
- 15,000 orders
- January 2024 to December 2025
- Indian cities and regions
- Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies, Home & Kitchen, and Beauty
- Sales, discounts, cost, profit, returns, shipping, payment and satisfaction fields

## Suggested KPIs
- Total Sales
- Net Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Orders
- Average Order Value
- Return Rate
- Average Customer Satisfaction

## Suggested dashboard pages
1. Executive Overview
2. Regional & City Performance
3. Product & Category Analysis
4. Customer & Segment Analysis
5. Operations: Shipping, Returns & Satisfaction

## Important
This dataset is synthetic and created for portfolio/learning purposes. It is not real company data.
